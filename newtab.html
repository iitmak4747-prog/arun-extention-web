⌁ Study activity trend

Meaning: each column is a saved calendar day (or month in yearly/all-time views). Teal = website time classified as Study; amber = Waste. A dash means no saved website entry, not proof of zero activity. Hover a column for its full date and exact totals.

✓ Task & habit completion

Meaning: Tasks is the share of currently saved tasks marked complete. Habits counts only explicit check-ins today. The cycle bar counts completed Pomodoro study phases in the selected period; its 8-cycle reference is visual, not a target.

◷ Focus session quality

Meaning: these are separate sources, not one combined total. Website Study/Waste comes from popup tracking, Pomodoro time comes from saved elapsed seconds, and manual logs are listed separately to prevent double-counting.

▤ Subject breakdown

Meaning: time grouped by a saved subject ID and label. Only records in subjectTime for this report range contribute; unlabelled time is not guessed or assigned to a subject.

⌃ Peak website study day

Meaning: the calendar day with the largest website time explicitly classified as Study in this report. It is not total Pomodoro time and it does not infer productivity outside tracked records.

◎ Data quality & recommendations

Meaning: counts show which saved data sources are available to this report. Missing history is marked unavailable; it is not fabricated or treated as missed effort.
